Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 5, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application iHoroscope, Daily Horoscope created by Play Indiga. This application is designed to provide information quickly and efficiently while respecting user privacy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our application is built with a "privacy-first" approach. We do not require users to login, register, or provide any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

  • JSON Data: The app fetches content from our servers in JSON format.
  • Caching: Fetched data is stored locally on your device (cache) to improve performance and enable offline viewing.

To keep this application free, we display advertisements from third-party providers. These providers may collect your device's Advertising ID to serve personalized ads.

While we do not collect personal data, we are committed to ensuring that the connection between the app and our JSON server is secure to prevent any unauthorized data interception.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
